C.L.E.A.R. is how you take the lead.

AI can help you draft, organize, compare, and learn. It still needs your direction—and you still need to check the result. CLEAR gives you a way to do both.

SAME TASK. VERY DIFFERENT DIRECTION.
VAGUE PROMPT

“Write an email.”

AI must guess the situation, audience, facts, and tone.
CLEAR PROMPT

“I'm helping a customer with a delayed order. Draft a friendly, four-sentence update using only these facts: the delivery is delayed and we'll confirm a new date tomorrow. Don't promise a date we don't know.”

You control the context, task, facts, audience, and review boundary.
